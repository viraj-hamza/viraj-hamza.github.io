Meet Viraj and Hamza

Chemistry · Medical admissions

Viraj Deorukhkar

Viraj is a Cambridge Natural Sciences graduate who specialised in Chemistry before progressing to Medicine at the University of Nottingham. Awarded the QIAGEN Scholarship at Lucy Cavendish College, he studied advanced organic, inorganic, physical and biological chemistry, with wider scientific training in Pathology and Pharmacology.

His scientific work extends beyond the degree course. Viraj conducted machine-learning research into asymmetric catalysis and co-authored a peer-reviewed Chemistry publication. Across four years of tutoring, he has helped students rebuild difficult topics, approach unfamiliar problems methodically and express their reasoning with the precision expected by examiners.

Having received medical-school offers from UCL, Manchester and Nottingham, he also supports UCAT preparation and interviews with insight from his own successful application cycle.

How we teach

Explanation is only the beginning.

Effective tutoring finds the precise point at which understanding breaks down — and then turns repaired understanding into independent performance.

  1. 01

    Diagnose

    We identify weak topics and hidden misconceptions through discussion, recent assessments and targeted questions.

  2. 02

    Rebuild

    We explain the underlying science clearly, adapting the pace and method to the individual student.

  3. 03

    Apply

    We move quickly into calculations, diagrams, data analysis and unfamiliar exam-style problems.

  4. 04

    Refine

    We teach students how marks are awarded, track progress and revisit weaker material deliberately.
