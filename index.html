How we teach

What a lesson looks like

We work out what the student is struggling with, teach it clearly, and then practise using it in exam questions.

  1. 01

    Find the gaps

    We use recent schoolwork, test results or a short set of questions to see where help is needed.

  2. 02

    Explain the topic

    We go back over the science at a pace that suits the student and check understanding as we go.

  3. 03

    Practise questions

    We work through calculations, diagrams, data and exam questions together.

  4. 04

    Review

    We look at what earned marks, what was missed and what should be covered next.

  5. 05

    Adapt and plan

    We use the student's feedback and progress to tailor future lessons and plan what to cover next.
